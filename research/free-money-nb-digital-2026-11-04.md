# Free money and programs for a one-person digital marketing business in New Brunswick (2026-11-04)

Public sources only. Dates noted. UNKNOWN where not found. Not advice. Most options are loans or repayable contributions, not pure grants.

## Summary
True non-repayable grants for a brand-new one-person digital marketing service business are rare. The practical options are loans (often with mentorship), income support like SEB, free advisory, or later-stage programs once there is revenue or export intent.

## 1. Futurpreneur Canada
- **What:** Collateral-free startup loan + up to 2 years of mentorship. Not a grant.
- **Amount:** Up to $75,000 total (typically up to $25k from Futurpreneur + up to $50k from BDC). Side Hustle stream up to $25k if keeping full-time income outside the business.
- **Who qualifies:** Canadian citizen or permanent resident, ages 18-39 at application, business in pre-startup or startup stage (generally under 24 months full-time operation). Must intend to work full-time (or meet Side Hustle rules).
- **Deadline:** Rolling / continuous. No fixed intake.
- **Link:** https://futurpreneur.ca/en/
- **Local:** Moncton contact listed on 211 (Chris Goguen, cgoguen@futurpreneur.ca; WorkSpace Moncton).
- **Note:** Age is the hard cutoff. Confirm current exact terms on the site.

## 2. CBDC Westmorland Albert (already researched)
- Loans up to $150,000 (General, Innovation, Youth, First Time Entrepreneur, Impact for Moncton/Dieppe/Riverview, etc.).
- Contact: 337 Main Street, Shediac; (506) 532-8312.
- Link: https://cbdc.ca/locations/cbdc-westmorland-albert/
- Best practical local lender for a rural or Greater Moncton one-person business that banks may not serve.

## 3. Opportunities NB (ONB)
- Provincial economic development agency. Free Business Navigator help to point you at options.
- Main tools for companies: payroll rebates (when creating jobs), loans, strategic assistance. Relationship-based, more useful once growing or hiring than for a pure one-person startup.
- Contact: info@onbcanada.ca; 506-453-5471; Place 2000, 250 King Street, Fredericton.
- Link: https://onbcanada.ca/businesses/ and https://www.gnb.ca/en/org/opportunities-nb.html
- Pure grant money for a tiny digital marketing startup: UNKNOWN / limited from public pages.

## 4. ACOA Business Development Program
- Interest-free repayable contributions for Atlantic SMEs. Can cover marketing, training, productivity, some capital.
- Amounts vary by project (examples cite $25k+). Better once there is a viable plan and preferably some track record.
- Link: ACOA site (search Business Development Program).

## 5. Export Funding NB
- Reimbursement up to 65% of eligible costs, max $15,000 (min project ~$5,000).
- Covers export-focused website additions, SEO, digital ads, social media strategy, market research, etc.
- Requires: NB head office, CRA business number, currently exporting or planning to export, growth in past 2 years. Excludes pure retail/wholesale and personal/social services in some descriptions.
- Link: https://learnsphere.ca/export-development/export-funding-nb
- Fit for pure local digital marketing services: weak until there is export intent and some history.

## 6. NBIF Innovation Voucher Fund
- Non-repayable, up to $80,000 covering ~80% of an eligible R&D or innovation project with a research partner.
- Priority to strategic sectors including ICT. Unlikely pure fit for a standard digital marketing service unless there is a clear technology or product development angle.
- Check current intake on NBIF site.

## 7. Other notes
- **Canada Digital Adoption Program (CDAP):** Closed. Boost stream closed Feb 2024; Grow Your Business Online closed Sep 2024; program wound down by March 31, 2025. No new applications in 2026.
- **Canada Small Business Financing Program:** Government-backed loans through banks (up to $1.15M). For qualifying small businesses. Not a grant.
- **BDC LIFT:** Preferential loans for digital/AI transformation (launched 2026). Loan, not grant.
- **SEED / student programs:** For students only; not applicable if not a student.

## Practical ranking for a one-person digital marketing business starting now
1. SEB (income support while starting) — already researched.
2. Futurpreneur if age 18-39 (loan + mentor).
3. CBDC First Time / Impact / Youth loan for working capital.
4. Free advisory from ONB Business Navigator or CBDC.
5. Later: ACOA or Export Funding NB once there is revenue and (for export) sales outside Canada.

Exact current amounts, interest rates, and approval odds: confirm with the program. No private details.
