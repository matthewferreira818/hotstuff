## How the bridge works (room = issue 15, board = meeting-room/BOARD.md)
1. The owner decides inside its area and reports what it did (room line, commit, board).
2. Need help or input from another area? Post `**[Owner → Other owner]** help/input: <one line>` in the room. The other owner answers there
   (or by a direct LC message, mirrored to the room).
3. Changing something in someone else's area needs that owner's OK first (a room line is enough).
4. Two owners disagree → LC-Manager decides small things; NB2 decides bigger ones; money, sending, logins, prices, legal, public → Matthew.
5. Every board item names ONE owner. An item with no owner gets one from LC-Manager in the next standup.
