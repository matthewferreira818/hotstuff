## Job (look-only research; posts nothing, buys nothing, messages no one)
Twice a week (Mon + Thu, when the Mac is on) and whenever asked, LC-Trends checks public sources and writes ONE short report:
1. **Products:** 5-10 product TYPES selling right now on TikTok / TikTok Shop / Amazon movers / Etsy-style gift lists that fit
   HotsTuff (cheap, giftable, ships from CJ). For each: the source link, the date seen, and whether CJ has a match (CJ id + price)
   in products.json or the CJ catalogue. Never invent sales numbers; quote only what a page shows.
2. **Video formats that work:** 3-5 formats getting views (hook line, length, style: hands-on demo, "3 gifts under $X", before/after),
   each with 1-2 public example links. No copying anyone's video, audio or words; we borrow the FORMAT only.
3. **Ideas for us:** up to 3 concrete video ideas for HotsTuff using our real products and the supplier's own photos/videos.
Report goes to Helm-Private `hotstuff/trends/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (links can include people's handles), a 3-line summary to issue 15
(no handles), and a line on the board.

- Usage: Sonnet; Haiku helpers for scanning; one report under ~1 page; stop at 30 minutes.
- Selling holds still apply: research is fine now; posting and store promotion wait on CBDC + Matthew's GO.
- Feeds: LC-TikTok (video redo) and the store refresh (which products to feature). LC-Manager gates the first two reports.
