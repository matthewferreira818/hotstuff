Help me send my East Coast Social outreach batch. You send these messages yourself from my own Facebook account, as the
page-message flow below describes. Read the rules first and follow them exactly.

**1. Read the rules and the batch** (open each, read it all):
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matthewferreira818/hotstuff/master/marketing/east-coast-social/outreach/README.md
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/matthewferreira818/hotstuff/master/marketing/east-coast-social/outreach/templates.md
- the newest `batch-YYYY-MM-DD.md` in https://github.com/matthewferreira818/hotstuff/tree/master/marketing/east-coast-social/outreach

**2. My footer** (use it exactly, at the bottom of every message, in the right language):
> Matthew Ferreira · East Coast Social · {MATTHEW'S MAILING ADDRESS: pasted by him each session, never stored in this repo} · (506) 889-9737 · findhotstuff.com/automation
> Not interested? Just reply "no thanks" and I won't message you again.
>
> (French: ... Pas intéressé? Répondez simplement « non merci » et je ne vous écrirai plus.)

**3. For each business in the batch, in order:**
- Open its Facebook page. Read the date of its most recent post.
- If it posted within the last month: do NOT send. Note "active" and move on.
- If messaging is turned off: do NOT send. Note it and move on.
- Otherwise send ONE message with the Message button on the page, using the template the batch names. Fill in the real business
  name and the real month of the last post. Add my footer. Do not change the offer, the prices, or any claim. Do not add
  anything that isn't in the template. Send it.
- Never message a personal profile, a group, or anyone who isn't the business's own page.

**4. Stop immediately and tell me** if Facebook shows any warning, checkpoint, "you can't send messages", CAPTCHA or
"unusual activity" notice, or if any page already has a reply from me or to me. Do not try to get around any of it.

**5. When done:** post ONE comment in https://github.com/matthewferreira818/hotstuff/issues/15 that starts with
`**[C.C → Claude]**` and lists, per business: sent / skipped (active, last-post month) / skipped (messaging off) / replied. Business
names only. No addresses or personal details. Then tell me the same list here.
