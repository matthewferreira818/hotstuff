# Etsy demand check — first pass, 2026-09-27

Four search angles run from the container. **Etsy blocked every page**
(HTTP 403, DataDome bot wall), and it blocked C.C in Matthew's Chrome on
the first search too. So nothing below was read off an Etsy page. Etsy
listing evidence is search-engine titles and summaries only. Free-tool
evidence is from pages actually fetched and read, except where it says
"snippet".

## The gate, half answered

- **Count: passes easily.** Search results surfaced 26–39 distinct Etsy
  listing IDs per angle for fee / profit / pricing calculator sheets
  (roughly 15+ separate sellers in the core query alone), and Etsy has
  auto-generated dozens of /market/ pages for variants of the phrase.
  Nowhere near "under ~5".
- **Recent sales: not seen.** No review dates, cart badges or sold counts
  were readable. Only Matthew's own phone can answer this half.

## The finding that matters more than the gate

**The headline promise is already free.** At least five web tools, read
directly, solve backwards for the lowest safe price with shipping and the
fixed processing fee included, no signup:

| Tool | What it does | URL |
|---|---|---|
| SaleCalc | Goal box: "To net $12, you should have a sale price of $13.75" | https://salecalc.com/etsy |
| MiniWebtool | Break-even price + reverse price finder for a target profit | https://miniwebtool.com/etsy-fee-calculator/ |
| SellerMath | "Break-even item price", Canada preset | https://contentforge-press.github.io/ |
| EtsyPricingCalc | "Build a price" mode, Canada option | https://etsypricingcalc.com/ |
| freeetsyfeecalculator.com | "Reverse: Target Profit → Price", CAD region | https://freeetsyfeecalculator.com/ |

The other tabs have free twins too: break-even ROAS (Listadum), monthly
projection (Sellfy), multi-product sheet (Craftybase, email-gated, skips
shipping). Etsy's own listing form has had an estimated-earnings
calculator since about March 2024 (profit at a price only — it does not
solve for the floor).

**Paid prices seen** (mostly snippet-grade, treat as rough): clustering at
**$1–$4**, heavy discounting; one fetched page at **$4 USD with 153
reviews** (Templatables); one snippet at ~$19.95 on sale; one at "$29
intro pricing". Off-Etsy premium: Paper and Spark, $97 USD, 118 reviews
(a full bookkeeping system, not a calculator).

**The only visible gap:** nobody seen bundles the minimum-profit floor, a
per-product LOSES/SAFE verdict across a product list, break-even ROAS and
a monthly view in one sheet the seller owns. Every piece of that exists
free on its own. That is an absence of evidence from thin snippets, not
proof.

## Demand signal

No public keyword volume found (Google Trends returned 429). Google
autocomplete offers "etsy fee calculator canada", "etsy profit calculator
google sheets", "…spreadsheet" — and "…free" as a top suggestion. 20+
companies maintain free Etsy calculators as lead magnets, several updated
within the last 60 days.

## Right Hand's read

$24 CAD is a paid version of something a buyer can get free in a browser
tab, in a field where comparable sheets sell for a few dollars. At $4 a
sale nets about $3 — it would take ~100 sales to reach one ECS month. The
council's GO (2026-09-26) was made without this. Recommendation logged
in DECISIONS.md.
