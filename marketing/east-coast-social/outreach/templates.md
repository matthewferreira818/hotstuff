session. English version of the footer (the address goes where `{ADDRESS}` is):

> Matthew Ferreira · East Coast Social · {ADDRESS} · (506) 889-9737 · findhotstuff.com/automation
> Not interested? Just reply "no thanks" and I won't message you again.

French version:

> Matthew Ferreira · East Coast Social · {ADDRESS} · (506) 889-9737 · findhotstuff.com/automation

Fill `[Business]` with the real name and `[month]` with the month of the page's real last post (C.C reads it). If the page has
posted within the last month, do not send.

## Why the wording is careful
Automatic posting to a client's Facebook page is not live yet (the checker's facts say Facebook is pending), and no client has
been served yet. So the messages never say "it posts to your page automatically". They name what is true today (my own store's
feed has published daily since August 7, with the link) and describe the service as what I would do for them.
