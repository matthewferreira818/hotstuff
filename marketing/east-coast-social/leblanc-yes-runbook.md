# LeBlanc says YES — the 10-minute launch, right at the counter

The follow-up's goal is a yes to the FREE week — not the $79 close. The close
happens after they've watched their page post itself for seven days. If the
yes lands, do THIS before leaving the building, from your phone:

## Step 1 — Their page, their ownership (3 min)
LeBlanc has NO Facebook page. Create it from THE OWNER'S account, not yours —
they own their page forever, we never hold a client's assets. Owner's phone:
facebook.com → Menu → Pages → Create. Name: **LeBlanc Restaurant** · Category:
Restaurant · Address: 583 rue Centrale, Memramcook · Hours: from the pack
(11:30–8 daily, Fri–Sat till 9 — confirm out loud while typing them in).

## Step 2 — You get posting access (2 min)
On their new page: Manage → Page access → **Invite Matthew Ferreira** with
task/editor access. They tap invite, you accept on your phone. That's the
whole "contract" for the free week.

## Step 3 — Day 1 goes live before you leave (2 min)
On your phone, open:
`findhotstuff.com/marketing/east-coast-social/engine/samples/leblanc-resto/day-1.png`
Save the image → post it on the new page with the Day 1 caption (below).
**This is the magic moment: their restaurant's first-ever Facebook post,
live, while they watch.**

## Step 4 — The week runs (your mornings, ~2 min each)
One card per morning, day-2 through day-7, same URL pattern (day-N.png).
Captions for all seven:
`findhotstuff.com/marketing/east-coast-social/engine/samples/leblanc-resto/captions.md`
Swap any price/item/hour the owner corrects at the counter — the pack sheet
says mockup prices get replaced with real ones at setup.

## If they need it to STOP (know this before you offer the week)

Nobody plans for the week they'll need this, so know the answer cold before
you're standing at a counter being asked.

**Their page, today:** you post it by hand every morning. Pausing is you not
posting. Instant, nothing to switch off. Text them: *"Saw the news. I've
stopped your posts — nothing goes up until you tell me. Take your time."*

**Our own channels** (if the reason is the whole town, not one shop): open
`github.com/matthewferreira818/hotstuff/new/master` on your phone, filename
`PAUSE`, body just `<date> MF` — the repo is public, so no names and no
reasons in it — commit to master. That silences the ECS Facebook page, X,
the product spotlights and TikTok by the next morning cron. The site feed
keeps running, which is what keeps the odometer honest. Delete the file to
resume. Your phone nags you daily until you do.

Already posted and it shouldn't have been? Pause first (60 seconds — the
next cron is what can still make it worse), then delete the post yourself.

Full version, including the billing rule and the wording to use with a
client: `marketing/east-coast-social/pause-procedure.md`.

## Step 5 — The close (day 8)
Walk back in: "That's what daily looks like. $79 a month and it keeps going —
you never touch it. No contract; cancel any month." Ask for the review while
the week's fresh either way.

## If it's a MAYBE
"No problem — when should I check back?" Log the date. Leave nothing extra;
the printed pack is already doing the selling on their counter.

## If it's a NO
Thank them warmly, leave it be, log it, rest the prospect. A no from a
neighbour handled gracefully is still marketing in Memramcook.
