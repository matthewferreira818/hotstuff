# East Coast Social — cold-call kit

_The phone channel: for businesses with no social page to DM, and DM ghosts
after the follow-up window. You call, personally — that's the brand.
Log every outcome in prospects.md after the call._

## When to call
- **Shops & services:** 10:00–11:30 AM (open, not yet slammed)
- **Restaurants & cafés:** 2:00–4:00 PM ONLY (never during a meal rush)
- Avoid Monday mornings everywhere. Friday afternoons are surprisingly good.

## The 30-second opener (sounds human, not scripted)

> Hi — is this the owner? My name's Matthew, I'm calling from Memramcook —
> so, basically a neighbour. I'll keep it to twenty seconds: I run a little
> local service that makes a business's Facebook page post every single day,
> automatically — you'd approve the look once and never touch it again.
> I noticed [you don't have a page / your page's been quiet since MONTH],
> and honestly that's money left on the table. Setup's free, it's $79 a
> month, and I'll build your first week of sample posts for nothing so you
> can see it with your own shop. Worth a coffee sometime this week?

**Key beats:** neighbour → 20 seconds → what it does → free to try → coffee.
If they're busy: "When's a better time? I'll call back — 30 seconds, promise."

## Walk-in playbook (hotels, no-page shops, DM ghosts)

**Bring:** 2 dark flyers, 3 business cards, phone with two tabs ready:
findhotstuff.com/automation and the @hotstuff2213 feed (live proof).

**Timing:** mid-morning (10–11) or mid-afternoon (2–4), midweek. For a
hotel, ask the front desk: "Is the GM or whoever handles marketing around?
Twenty seconds, I promise." Get a NAME even if they're out.

**The pitch (Hôtel Shediac / Bistro Blonde version):** "Your hotel page is
actually great — but Bistro Blonde hasn't posted in about two years, and
that's a restaurant in the middle of Shediac's tourist season. I run a
local service that gives it its own daily voice, automatically — free
setup, $79 a month, and I'll build the first week of sample posts free so
you can see it before deciding." Show the store feed scrolling. Hand over
flyer + card.

**The close:** "Who should I email the one-pager to?" — a name + email is
a WIN even without a yes. Log every walk-in outcome in prospects.md.
