The honest part about Google

I can't promise Google will approve your listing, and I'd be lying if I said otherwise.

Google verifies businesses by postcard, phone, or a video walkthrough, and they decide — not me. It usually takes a few days. Sometimes it takes weeks, and occasionally they ask you to do it twice.

What I promise is the part I control: everything filed correctly the first time, the video shot the way Google actually asks for it, and me on the phone with you until it goes through. If Google ends up refusing your listing, the Facebook side still stands and I'll take $100 off, no argument.

A Google Business Profile is free from Google — you never pay Google anything. What you're paying me for is the work: filing it correctly, building the page, and standing behind it until it's live.

I'd rather tell you that up front than have you find out after you've paid.
