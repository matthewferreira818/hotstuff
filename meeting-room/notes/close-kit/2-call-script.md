# 2. The 10-minute call

Matthew makes every call (calls to individual people are his). This is a read-off-the-screen script.
Best times: shops and services 10:00-11:30 AM; restaurants and cafés 2:00-4:00 PM. Not Monday mornings.
Goal: **leave the call with one yes** (to the samples, or to the pilot if they've seen samples). Not a contract, not a payment.

## Before the call (2 minutes)
- [ ] Open their website/Facebook page and /automation in two tabs.
- [ ] Know where they are: no samples yet, or samples sent on (date).
- [ ] Have their private notes open (Helm-Private `ecs/`), never on screen-share.

## The script

**0:00 Hello (30 sec)**
> "Hi [NAME], it's Matthew from East Coast Social, in Memramcook. You replied to my email about Facebook posts. Got ten minutes, or is there a better time?"

Busy? "When's better? I'll call back then." Write it down, hang up.

**0:30 Their business first (4 min).** Ask, don't pitch. Their answers ARE the posts. Write them down.

**4:30 What it is (2 min)**
> "Here's all it is. I make you 7 sample posts first, free, from your own details. Nothing goes on your page and you keep them.
> If you like them, I post once a day on your Facebook page for a first pilot month, $79. You see each week's posts first and approve them.
> After that it's $79 a month only if we both want to keep going. No contract, and you can text me STOP any day."

**6:30 Their questions (2 min).** Use the answers in [1-reply-playbook.md](1-reply-playbook.md). Don't know? "Good question, let me check and text you today." Never guess.

**8:30 The ask (1 min)**
- No samples yet: "Want me to make your 7 sample posts? You'd have them within a week."
- Samples seen: "Want to start the pilot month? I'd send a few quick questions and you'd add me to your page from your own account."

**9:30 Close (30 sec).** Repeat back the next step and the day: "So I'll send the samples by [day]." Thank them.

## What to ask (checklist; tick what you got)
- [ ] What do you sell or do most? Top 3 things.
- [ ] Who are your customers, and how do they usually find you?
- [ ] Slow days or slow season?
- [ ] Anything coming up: a special, an event, a "every Thursday" deal?
- [ ] Do you post now? Who does it? (Keep them: we run underneath.)
- [ ] Photos: which ones are OK to use? Staff faces OK?
- [ ] Anything never to post (prices, competitors, names)?
- [ ] Who approves posts, and text or email?
- [ ] Best time of day for posts?
- [ ] English, French, or both?

## Never on the call
- No discounts or new prices (Matthew decides price, and only off-call).
- No promises of reach, sales, followers or reviews.
- No "free setup for the first two" or "spots are limited".
- Never ask for a password or card number. Payment comes later, by e-Transfer, after they approve week 1.

## After the call (5 minutes)
- [ ] Write what they said into their private folder (Helm-Private `ecs/`), today.
- [ ] Do the next step you promised, on the day you said.
- [ ] A "no" is a complete result: log it, never call again.
