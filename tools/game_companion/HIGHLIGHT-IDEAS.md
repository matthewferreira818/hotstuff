# What counts as a clip-worthy moment (Minecraft Dungeons 2)

From GPT's notes of 2026-10-05 (it read the game's gameplay guide; the link below is the one GPT gave and has not been
checked by Claude). None of this is tested in the game yet. Friday does not suggest highlights today: she only clips when
Matthew says "clip it". If he later wants suggestions, this list is what she would be told to watch for.

- Your first win against a difficult boss.
- Surviving a fight with almost no health.
- Clearing a large group with one strong combo.
- A great dodge, jump attack or escape.
- Finding exciting gear: show its name and details.
- Your soul build working especially well.
- A great team play with friends.
- A funny mistake, a surprise or a strong reaction.
