# Friday: setup and first jobs (plan for 2026-10-25)

2026-10-24 · Claude, for Matthew ("the plan tomorrow is to configure Friday a lot more and get her tasks responsibly").
Public file: no keys, no private details. Everything here is free and uses what The Helm already has.

## The idea: a job ladder

Friday earns bigger jobs by doing smaller ones well. Each rung is one try, watched by Matthew, before it becomes routine.
She moves up a rung only after **3 clean runs in a row** (did the job, asked when unsure, no wrong clicks).

| Rung | What she does | Risk | Example jobs |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Look and tell | Reads the screen or a page and reports back. No hands. | None | "What does this page say?", "Summarize today's Meeting Room", "Check the board for anything waiting on me" |
| 2. Find | Opens a search or link in his browser and reads it. | Very low | "Find the Minecraft wiki page for X", "Open the store's automation page and read me today's card" |
| 3. Tidy clicks | Hands on, ordinary clicks and scrolling, nothing that sends. | Low | "Scroll this to the bottom", "Click the next video", "Close these tabs" |
| 4. Hand to C.C. | Gives C.C. a vetted command; the Allow box shows the full words. | Medium | "Tell C.C. to run the daily posting session" |
| 5. Post or send | Only words Matthew gave her exactly; every Post press is his Allow. | High | Typing an approved post into a box he has open |

Never on the ladder, whatever the rung: logins and passwords, payments and banks, Moomoo, System Settings, terminals,
the app itself, spending money, secrets. Those stay his own hands (CLAUDE.md).

## Setup checklist (about 15 minutes, in The Helm)

1. **Brain for the Helper:** Google's free tier for jobs while gaming; the local model (gemma3:4b) for private jobs when the Mac is idle.
2. **What she sees:** choose "all screens" or "just the window I pick" in Settings. All screens means banking tabs or messages on screen go to Google too.
3. **Hands switch:** leave it off by default; turn it on only for a job. Check the off-limits list is still there.
4. **Teach C.C.'s box** once (hold the mouse on C.C.'s message box for six seconds), so rung 4 works without copy-paste.
5. **Usage:** keep the default Low usage; the free tier has daily limits.
