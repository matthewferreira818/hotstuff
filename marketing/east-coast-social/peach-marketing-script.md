# Peach Marketing — the call, and why it isn't the pitch we planned

_Written 2026-09-01. Read the "what changed" section before you dial._

---

## What changed after research

The white-label list put Peach in Tier A because they're a real local agency
15 minutes away. Then Claude read their actual site. Their client list:

**Université de Moncton · New Brunswick Tourism · Titleist · ANBL ·
Destination Moncton-Dieppe · Pays de la Sagouine · Kent Regional Service
Commission · Louisbourg Mines Museum · Chalmers Foundation · UNI**

That is provincial tourism, a national golf brand, and a university. Their
tagline is **"Creating Together."** This is not a shop that will resell a
$79/mo daily-posting engine to barbershops — they'd have no client to put it
on, and pitching it that way makes you look like you didn't do your homework.

**So the pitch flips.** You are not selling Peach anything. You are offering
to take the work they have to turn down.

An agency serving NB Tourism gets calls every month from a hair salon or a
pizza place asking "can you do our Facebook?" They have to say no — those
clients can't carry an agency's rates. Right now those people get nothing.

**You are where Peach sends them.** That costs Peach zero, makes them look
generous to a local business they just declined, and sends you warm,
pre-qualified leads from a name people in Dieppe respect.

That's worth more to you than white-labeling. Your cold calls run 5-to-1
against you. A referral from Peach walks in already trusting you.

## Before you call: two minutes of homework

Open **peachmarketing.ca** and actually look at the Destination
Moncton-Dieppe case study. Not to flatter them — so the sentence in the
script below is **true when you say it.** If you don't look, cut that line.
Same rule as everything else we publish: no claim we can't stand behind.

**Also worth knowing:** their own site lists no phone number and no email —
only a contact form. The number below came from a business directory, not
from them. If it's dead, the contact form is the real door.

## The call (roughly 40 seconds)

**Number:** (506) 382-8063 — *directory-sourced, confirm it's them when
someone picks up*
**Address:** 50 rue du Marché, Suite 210, Dieppe — *the same building as
Boulangerie Tony from your round-1 call sheet*
**When:** mid-morning, 10:00–11:30, midweek. Agencies are useless Monday
mornings and gone by 4.

> Hi — my name's Matthew Ferreira, I'm out in Memramcook. I'm not calling to
> sell you anything, I promise. Is whoever handles new business around?

*(when you get them)*

> Thanks for taking a second. I looked at your Destination Moncton-Dieppe
> work — you're playing at a level I'm not, and that's actually why I'm
> calling.
>
> I run a small thing called East Coast Social. All it does is post to a
> local business's Facebook page every day, automatically, for seventy-nine
> a month. It's the low end of the market — it's the client you have to say
> no to.
>
> My guess is you get a few calls a month from a salon or a pizza place who
> can't afford an agency, and right now you've got nowhere to send them.
> I'd like to be that place. Costs you nothing, you look good for having an
> answer, and I'll tell them you sent me.
>
> If it helps, my own store's feed has published a new post every single day
> for months — findhotstuff.com/automation, you can go look at it right now.
> That's the whole demo.
>
> Worth me sending you a one-pager so you've got it on file?

**The close is a name and an email**, not a yes. "Who should I send it to?"
A contact at Peach on file is a win even if nothing happens for six months.

## If they push back

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We don't really refer out." | "Fair enough. Can I leave you my number anyway? Costs you nothing to have it in a drawer for the next salon that calls." |
| "What's your background?" | **Be straight.** "I'm not an agency guy — I've got a day job. I built the posting system myself over the last few months and I run it on my own store. That's why it's $79 and not $1,500." *Honest beats impressive here. They'll check anyway.* |
| "Are you looking for work?" | Only you can answer that. If yes, say yes — it's a real door and an agency that likes you is worth more than one referral. |
| "Send it to info@..." | Take it, thank them, and **send it the same day.** |
| "We have someone we already refer to." | "No problem at all — thanks for the straight answer." Log it and move on. That's a complete result. |
