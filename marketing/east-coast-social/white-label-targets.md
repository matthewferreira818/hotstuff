## The pitch (30 seconds, same shape as the cold-call opener)

> Hi — I'm Matthew, I'm in Memramcook. I'm not selling you anything, I'm
> offering you something to resell. I built an engine that posts to a
> business's social page every single day, automatically — it's been running
> on my own store's feed without missing a day, and you can go look at it
> right now at findhotstuff.com/automation. It costs me nothing to run.
>
> I'd rather have you sell it to your clients than keep cold-calling barber
> shops. You white-label it, you charge whatever you want, I take a cut.
> Want me to run a free week on one of your clients so you can see it?

**The close is the same as always:** not "do you want it," but *"give me one
client to run free for a week."* The sample pack is the whole sales pitch —
it works on agencies exactly like it works on restaurants.

## Two honest risks before you dial

1. **You'd be showing your engine to people who could copy it.** They can't
   copy the 4 months of wiring, but they can copy the idea in an afternoon.
   Pitch the *result and the free running cost*, not the how.
2. **Agencies ask harder questions than barbers.** "What happens if you get
   hit by a bus," "what's your uptime," "who owns the client relationship."
   Have an answer for the third one before you call: **they do.** You never
   touch their client. That's the whole appeal.
