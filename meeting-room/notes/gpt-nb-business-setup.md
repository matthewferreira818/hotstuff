## 1. Business name

Service New Brunswick says a person trading under a business name normally registers it using Form 5, within two months of starting. Its instructions call for a New Brunswick NUANS name search first, and renewal every five years. Registration has a fee; these notes do not approve spending. Registering a name and incorporating are different choices. [SNB instructions](https://www.pxw1.snb.ca/snb7001/b/1000/CSS-FOL-SNB-45-0003B.pdf), [SNB overview](https://www2.snb.ca/content/snb/en/sites/corporate-registry/business-and-partnership-names.html).

Ask SNB how the rules apply to each trading name and the actual start date. A domain or social account does not establish that registration was completed. Local permits may also apply; the federal startup guide points to provincial requirements and the government permit finder. [Federal startup guide](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/business/start/register-with-gov/register-sole-prop-partner.html).

## 2. Business number

A federal business number (BN) identifies the business or legal entity. CRA says an unincorporated business needs one when registering for a CRA program account, such as GST/HST or payroll. New accounts attach to an existing BN; do not create another for the same entity. A BN alone does not show that GST/HST registration is active. [CRA: when a BN is needed](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/business-registration/business-number-program-account/need-bn.html).

The newer CRA page is the stronger current reference, but check the actual registration confirmation with SNB/CRA before applying. Do not put confirmation documents or numbers in this repo. ## 3. GST/HST

For most ordinary businesses, the small-supplier limit is $30,000 CAD of worldwide taxable revenue before expenses, including zero-rated sales and associates’ revenue. For a sole proprietor, combine all their businesses; it is not a separate allowance for each brand. Financial services, capital-property sales and goodwill sales are excluded.

- Exceed $30,000 in one calendar quarter: charge tax on the sale that crosses the limit and register within 29 days of the effective registration date.
- Exceed it across four or fewer consecutive quarters, but not in one quarter: small-supplier status ends at the end of the month after that quarter. Start charging on the first taxable supply after losing that status; register within 29 days of the effective date.
- Stay within the limit: registration is generally optional for taxable businesses. Voluntary registration is available; check its obligations before choosing it.

These are the general rules, not a decision about this business’s tax status. [CRA threshold rules and examples](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/when-register-charge.html).

## 4. Leads and privacy

PIPEDA generally covers personal information used in commercial activity in NB, including information crossing borders. NB’s similar provincial law concerns health information. There is a narrow exception for business contact details used solely to communicate about someone’s work; do not assume every lead-form field qualifies. [Privacy Commissioner overview](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-laws-in-canada/the-personal-information-protection-and-electronic-documents-act-pipeda/pipeda_brief/).

A privacy notice should match the real collection and handling: what is collected, why, who receives it, and meaningful risks. Show the key points when collecting information, make the full policy easy to find, and distinguish necessary use from optional purposes. [Meaningful consent guidance](https://www.priv.gc.ca/en/privacy-topics/privacy-for-businesses/appropriate-handling-of-personal-information/collecting-personal-information-and-consent/consent/gl_omc_201805/).

For review here: map the lead form, Worker and phone-alert relay; decide who has access, retention, safeguards and how someone can ask for access or correction. Public GitHub must never hold leads, client records or payload logs. These are review questions, not a finished privacy policy.

Marketing email/text also needs a separate CASL check: consent or an applicable exception, sender information and an unsubscribe method. Do not treat a callback request as blanket permission for later campaigns. [CRTC FAQ](https://www.crtc.gc.ca/eng/com500/faq500.htm).

## 5. Client agreement and records

A contract can be spoken or written. Federal consumer guidance explains the value of clear responsibilities and cancellation terms; it is not a ready-made business-to-business agreement. [ISED contract guide](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/office-consumer-affairs/en/modern-marketplace/contracts).

Discussion points for a short ECS service agreement:

- Actual platforms and posting frequency; sample approval and who presses Publish.
- Price in CAD, tax treatment, billing dates, cancellation and account handover.
- Client permission for photos and claims; account ownership and revocable access.
- Data handling and what happens if a channel stops working; no promised sales results.
