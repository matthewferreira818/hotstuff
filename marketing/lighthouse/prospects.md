## How to work this list (10 min/day)
1. Pick 3-5 names, check their IG/FB feed age on your phone.
2. Mark them below: 🎯 dead feed / ✅ active (skip) / ❓ no page found (also a
   prospect — "I'll build your page AND keep it alive").
3. DM or walk in — script at the bottom. 5 outreaches/day max, always
   personalized with their actual last-post month.
