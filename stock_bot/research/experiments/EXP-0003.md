### Why costs decide day trading

With $100 bets a round trip costs about $2.20 ($1 each way plus slippage), so a trade has to move about **+2.2% just to break even**. With $1,000 bets it's about +0.3%. Day-trading moves are small, so the bet size matters as much as the strategy.

### Caveats

- 60 days is a short sample, and a single market mood.
- Fills on 5-minute closes; real day-trading fills are usually worse in fast moves.
- Stocks chosen from today's screen of big swingers.
- Taxes: frequent day trading is what CRA treats as business income, even in a TFSA.
