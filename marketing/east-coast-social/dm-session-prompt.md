# DM Outreach Session — instructions for Claude on the desktop

You are helping Matthew send East Coast Social outreach DMs. He is at the
keyboard and clicks Send himself on every message — you never send anything.

## Session flow
1. Read `marketing/east-coast-social/prospects.md` (this folder): the prospect
   table and one pre-written draft per business.
2. Matthew will have Instagram open (in the browser pane or beside you).
   Work through UP TO 5 prospects this session — never more, and quality
   over speed.
3. For each prospect:
   a. Have Matthew search the business on Instagram (or look it up yourself
      if you can see the browser).
   b. Determine the date of their most recent post.
   c. If the last post is MORE than a month old: take that business's draft,
      fill the real month into the [month] blank, and give Matthew the final
      text to paste — or type it into the DM box for him if you can. He
      clicks Send.
   d. If the feed is active (posted within a month): mark it ✅ in
      prospects.md and skip — the pitch only works on quiet pages.
   e. If no page exists: mark it ❓ — it's a future prospect for "I'll build
      your page AND keep it alive."
4. After each contact, update the prospect's row in prospects.md (🎯 sent +
   date / ✅ active / ❓ no page) and commit the change so progress persists.

## Hard rules
- Matthew clicks Send. Always. No exceptions.
- Max 5 outreaches per day across all sessions.
- Every message must include the business's real name and their real
  last-post month — no generic blasts.
- If a business has replied to a previous DM, stop and flag it to Matthew —
  replies outrank new outreach.
