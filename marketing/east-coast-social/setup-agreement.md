## About Google — required disclosure

**A Google Business Profile is free. Google never charges you for it, and you
can create and manage one yourself at no cost.** What you are paying me for is
my labour: building it, filing it correctly, and standing beside you through
verification.

**I cannot promise Google will approve your listing.** Google decides, not me.
Verification is usually a live video walkthrough captured inside Google's own
tool, it commonly takes days and sometimes weeks, and it can fail.

**If your listing is not verified 30 days after we first submit it, that half
of the job closes and you owe nothing for it.** You keep the Facebook page and
everything else, and you pay $150 only.
