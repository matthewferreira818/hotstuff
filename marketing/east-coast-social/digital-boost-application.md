## The two-path strategy

- **NOW — apply as a Business (recipient).** Any NB-based business is
  welcome to apply; funded projects must involve AI, Data, or
  Cybersecurity. The program pairs you with an approved provider and funds
  their work for you. Free expert help + funding for the stack.
- **LATER — apply as a Solution Provider.** East Coast Social qualifies on
  paper (Atlantic-based, real AI expertise) EXCEPT providers need **two
  customer references** with sample deliverables. The moment ECS has two
  happy clients, we submit the provider application — then Digital Boost
  literally sends funded clients to ECS. This is the big prize.
