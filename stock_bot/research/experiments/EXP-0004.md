### Caveats

- 60 days is a short sample and one market mood.
- Fills on 5-minute closes. Cheap stocks have wider bid/ask spreads, so real fills are usually worse than 0.1%.
- The stock list comes from today's universe (survivorship bias).
- US rule: in a margin account under US$25,000, more than 3 day trades in 5 business days can get the account restricted (pattern day trader rule). A cash account avoids that but must wait for sold money to settle (1 business day) before reusing it. Check Moomoo's current rules before any real money.
- Taxes: frequent day trading is what CRA treats as business income, even in a TFSA.
