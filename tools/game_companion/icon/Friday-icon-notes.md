## Original generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE original production macOS app icon for The Helm, whose assistant Friday also acts as a defensive security guard. 1024×1024 RGBA PNG. Center an approximately 824×824 rounded-square obsidian glass body on a fully transparent canvas, leaving about 100 px transparent margin. Premium glossy near-black and deep charcoal glass with dimensional reflections. Accent palette is CRIMSON RED ONLY: deep ruby, dark blood-red glass and sharp red highlights. No cyan, teal, blue, green, purple or orange light. Modern, powerful hacker aesthetic; sophisticated and clean, not a retro terminal. Straight-on, no perspective, no scene, no pedestal. Main emblem must be broad, bold and readable at 16px, about 500px wide, with little fine detail. No words, labels, captions, watermark, particles or detached pixels. Keep all glow and reflections inside the rounded-square body; absolutely transparent outside its clean silhouette. Original art only; no familiar game logos, Overwatch, circle with triangle/arrow, compass/navigation arrows, Iron Man/Marvel, Guy Fawkes/Anonymous mask, skull-and-crossbones or traced design. Emblem: a faceless hacker hood made from two broad sculptural folds of translucent smoky-black glass outlined with luminous crimson edges. Rounded hood crown and tapered shoulders form one compact silhouette. The face is a deep clean shadow void with NO eyes, face, mouth, circuitry or human likeness. A small angular crimson inner fold hints at intelligence without depicting a person. Large, confident, iconic, elegant and original.
