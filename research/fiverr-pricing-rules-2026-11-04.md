# Fiverr pricing and rules check (2026-11-04)

Public sources only. Dates noted. UNKNOWN where not found.

## Pricing reality for new sellers (0 reviews)

- Platform minimum is $5. Some categories have higher package minimums (e.g. full website ~$80 in some guides). [sellerosforfiverr.com guide, Oct 2026]
- New sellers are commonly advised to price at the lower end of a fair range for the first 5–10 orders to get reviews, not at the absolute floor. Typical starter advice: Basic $10–25 range for many services, then raise after reviews. [globehustle.co.uk, Oct 2026; fiverrfusion.com, Aug 2026]
- Exact current prices charged by new sellers for (a) 30-day social posts, (b) Google Business Profile, (c) one-page websites: UNKNOWN from public search results in this pass. Top gigs in these categories often include revisions, fast delivery, and sample work or portfolios.
- Fiverr takes 20% of the order (seller keeps 80%). Client-side fees exist but are separate. [etemora.com review, Oct 2026; seller OS guide]

## How new sellers get first orders

- Competitive (not rock-bottom) pricing for the first orders.
- Strong portfolio samples or spec work, clear specific titles/descriptions.
- Fast response rate (measured on first replies within 24h).
- Buyer Requests / Briefs and external sharing of the gig link (own social, communities). [Multiple 2026 guides]
- New accounts are limited to ~4 active gigs. [seller OS guide, Oct 2026]

## Rules that matter

- **AI use:** Allowed across categories if responsible, legal, and used to support (not replace) the seller’s skill. Final work must be refined and customized. Disclosure is not required in the gig by default, but must be given if a client asks or states a no-AI preference. Seller is fully accountable. [Fiverr Help Center: Using AI on Fiverr; Community Standards on AI]
- **Contact info / off-platform:** Do not share email, phone, or other contact details for the purpose of doing business off Fiverr. All payments must stay on-platform. [Fiverr Community Standards]
- **Canada payouts / tax:** Canadian residents must complete a DAC7 tax form within 67 days of notification or risk suspension and blocked withdrawals. [Fiverr Help Center: Tax Regulations for Canada]
- **Verification:** Some new freelancers may be asked for ID verification (possible one-time fee; amount not published). [seller OS guide]
