## Reference apps (borrow the feel, not the look; no copying names, logos or art)
- **ChatGPT's voice mode / Pi:** one screen, one orb, one big "tap to talk". Nothing else competes.
- **Apple Home / Weather:** a few big glanceable cards, plain words, detail only when you tap.
- **Duolingo:** one clear "next thing to do", friendly words, a short first-run tour.
- **Calm / Headspace:** calm colours, lots of space, one action per screen.

## Simple mode = the default
- **Home has only:** Friday's orb, ONE big button "🎙️ Talk to Friday", and at most 3 cards:
  1. "Needs you" (things waiting for his OK, plain sentence each, with Yes / Not now),
  2. "Today" (2-3 lines: what the crew is doing, in plain words),
  3. "Your businesses" (store + ECS, one line each, real numbers only).
- **Four big tabs at most**, plain names: Home · Friday · Store · Messages. Everything else (stock robots, ECS details,
  hacking area, crew station, Helper, Council, Lab, Guard, Launchpad, Switchboard) goes under **More**, each with a
  one-line "what this is" under its name.
- **No jargon on screen:** no "deck", "switchboard", "launchpad", "relay", "gate", branch names, commit numbers.
  Say what it does ("Let Claude wake your Mac helpers").
- **Bigger text and buttons**, lots of space, calm colours; nothing scrolls on Home.
- **First-run tour:** 4-5 skippable cards ("This is Friday. Tap here to talk", "Here's what needs you", ...),
  re-openable from More → "Show me around".
- **"?" bubbles:** one-sentence help on anything not obvious.
- **Advanced mode** switch (in More → Settings) brings back today's full Signal Deck for when he wants it.
